3 · Wheels & tires

Big rim helper:
Rim size is a signed byte: 1–127 are large (the community used 92), and 128–255 read as negative, which makes tiny wheels. Thin tires set the width and sidewall to 0 so the tire stops growing with the rim. Stock range is 14–28.
Untested in game by me. Very large rims have frozen the vinyl shop in other people’s saves. Keep a backup and test in free roam first.

4 · Lights

Each car has 19 light entries. A is the bulb or front colour and B is the cover or rear colour. Stock headlights are entries with brightness 4.0. The mode number is the light’s role: 0 and 1 are the headlights, and the rest cover brake, reverse, police and so on. Values from the codebooks: FFFFFFFD is bright flashing, FFFFFFFF flashing, FFFFFFFE faster flashing, FFFFFFF3 no light.
